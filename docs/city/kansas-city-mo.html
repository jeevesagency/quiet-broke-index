What's distinctive about Kansas City's squeeze

Kansas City is one of the most slack-rich metros in the index. A $400K household keeps roughly $180,960 a year after taxes and the four major fixed costs — closer to $181k of breathing room than most of America's coasts can dream of. The thing to be honest about: comfort here is mostly geography, not virtue. The same gross in San Francisco or New York would feel completely different.
