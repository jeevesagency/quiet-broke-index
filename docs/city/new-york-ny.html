What's distinctive about New York's squeeze

New York's squeeze isn't one thing — it's two. The median 3BR runs $4,900/month (14.7% of a $400K gross, vs. 8.3% for the 30-metro median), and the combined effective tax rate at $400K MFJ is 42.1% — 3.8 points above the median metro. After the five fixed costs and taxes, the slack on a $400K HHI here is about $96,720 a year — real money, but a long way from "rich."
