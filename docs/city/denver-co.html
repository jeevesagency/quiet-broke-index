What's distinctive about Denver's squeeze

Denver ranks 13 of 30 not because any single line item is brutal, but because all five are above-average at once. Housing is $3,050/month, childcare runs $1,720/kid, and the combined effective tax rate is 37.9%. None of these alone would make the news. Stacked, they consume 60.8% of a $400K gross. After the five fixed costs and taxes, the slack on a $400K HHI here is about $156,720 a year — real money, but a long way from "rich."
